# Companion APK download content review

Run ID: `content-review-20260731-companion-download-final`

Scope: new and changed English copy on the Companion install/download panel, the `Companion` navigation
label, and the installation guidance added to `README.md`, `DEPLOY.md`, and `companion/README.md`.

Reviewer lens: independent product content strategist in four delta-scoped passes. Later passes reviewed only
the replacements introduced by the immediately preceding verdict.

Verdict: **APPROVED**

- `Install Accrawl Companion`
- `The Accrawl Companion installs on Android. Download the APK on your Android phone, then open the downloaded file to install it. Android may ask you to allow installs from this source.`
- `Download for Android`
- `Not on your Android phone? Scan this code with your phone to open the same download.`
- `QR code linking to the official Accrawl Companion APK download`
- `Open Accrawl Companion on the Android phone you want to pair.`

The content strategist replaced “this browser” with Android's more accurate “this source,” made the on-phone
download requirement explicit for desktop readers, explained that the QR opens the same download, and gave the
QR alternative text an equivalent destination. The visible download link remains the accessible fallback.
